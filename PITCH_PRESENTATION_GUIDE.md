## 2. Key Conversion Features Built Into the Website

1. **Pune Daily Live Silver Rate Ticker & Calculator**
   - Pune shoppers check silver prices every morning.
   - The interactive calculator allows customers to select weight (e.g., 50g, 100g, 1kg), purity (99.9% vs 92.5%), and article type (Pooja, Dining, Jewellery).
   - Generates transparent estimates and lets them click **"Inquire This Rate on WhatsApp"** directly to `088887 24444`.

2. **Silversmith Artisanship Positioning (Not Just Retailers)**
   - Highlights their true craft: **Custom Temple Kalash, Deity Mukut, Silver Armor (Kavach), and Antique Restoration**.
   - Generates high-margin custom orders from temple trusts and affluent families in Pune.

3. **Turning the 3.9 Google Rating into an Advantage**
   - Highlights the authentic customer praise: *"Happy for price and best conversation with sales person."*
   - Prominent **"Review Us on Google"** button with a direct link. By having in-store shoppers scan this website to drop a review, their rating will rapidly rise to **4.7+ stars**.

4. **1-Click WhatsApp Direct Lead Capture**
   - Every single product card, rate calculation, and consultation form directly launches WhatsApp pre-filled with the product name and weight to `088887 24444`.
   - Zero middleman fees or aggregator commissions.

5. **Marathi & English Language Switcher**
   - Seamless 1-click toggle between English and Marathi (`मराठी`), catering both to young modern buyers and traditional Pune elders.

6. **Warje NDA Road Location & Hours Clarity**
   - Highlights "Open Daily until 9:30 PM" with an active live pulse indicator.
   - Interactive Google Map directions, parking guide, and landmarks (Giridhar Nagar, NDA Road).
